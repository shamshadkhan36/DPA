Residential • 3BHK • Thane West

The Vasant Serene

A contemporary 3BHK home featuring warm neutral palettes, subtle acoustic fluting, integrated indirect illumination, and bespoke teak cabinetry.

Design Concept

Serenity Through Material Harmony

The homeowners wanted an uncluttered, modern sanctuary away from the hustle of Thane city. We opened up the living and dining threshold to maximize natural light and framed the space with muted champagne gold metal accents, Italian travertine textures, and soft charcoal undertones.

Every storage element was flush-mounted with push-to-open hardware, ensuring visual continuity across all three bedrooms while accommodating expansive storage needs.

Project Metadata

Location:Thane West, MMR
