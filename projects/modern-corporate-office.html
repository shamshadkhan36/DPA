Commercial • Office • Andheri, Mumbai

Zenith Headquarters

A high-productivity corporate office featuring acoustic breakout zones, glass executive cabins, and ergonomic collaborative desks.

Design Concept

Agile Collaboration & Brand Authority

Designed for a rapidly scaling technology consultancy in Andheri East, the brief demanded a corporate environment that balances privacy for intense executive focus with energetic collaborative spaces for creative teams.

We installed double-glazed black aluminum acoustic demountable partitions, ceiling sound baffles, integrated cable trays, and biophilic indoor plant walls.

Project Metadata

Location:Andheri East, Mumbai
Capacity:45 Workstations + 4 Cabins
Carpet Area:2,400 sq. ft.
Scope:Turnkey Commercial Design & Fit-Out
Timeline:60 Days
